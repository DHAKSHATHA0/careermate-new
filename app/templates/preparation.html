The STAR Framework for Behavioral Interviews

Structure your answers to behavioral questions with confidence and clarity using the standard four-step technique:

S — Situation: Describe the context, timeline, and background of the challenge.
T — Task: Outline your specific role, goal, and responsibility.
A — Action: Explain the exact tools, methodologies, and decisions you executed.
R — Result: Quantify the outcome, metrics improved, and key lessons learned.

Essential HR Questions

"Tell me about yourself."

Focus 80% on recent technical achievements, projects, and target career alignment.

"Why do you want to join our company?"

Reference specific engineering products, culture, or technical challenges they are tackling.

"Describe a difficult technical bug or conflict you resolved."

Use STAR framework. Highlight collaboration, root-cause debugging, and tests added.
